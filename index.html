What we make

Parts under the vehicle, matched to the number on your parts list

01 · Driveline

Drive Shafts

Complete propeller shafts and intermediate shafts with universal joints, sliding splines and centre bearings.

  • GAZ, UAZ, LADA, KAMAZ & PAZ applications
  • 4WD propshafts for Korean & Japanese SUVs
02 · Driveline

CV Axles & Half Shafts

Front and rear half shafts with outer CV joints, tripod joints and boots — left and right variants identified.

  • Left / right length differences respected
  • ABS ring versions available
03 · Steering

Steering Shafts & Gears

Steering column cardan shafts, intermediate shafts and rack-and-pinion steering gears, with and without power assist.

  • Manual & power-steering versions kept separate
  • RHD / LHD applications
04 · Components

Universal Joints & Yokes

Cross joints, slip yokes, flange yokes and end yokes in the common OE sizes used across truck and off-road drivelines.

  • Sealed needle bearings, pre-greased
05 · Components

Splined Shafts & Couplings

Spline shafts, stub shafts, couplings and steering knuckle components machined to the original tooth profile.

  • Involute and straight splines
06 · Service

OEM / ODM & Private Label

Manufacturing against your drawing or sample, with your branding on the part and the packaging.

  • Reverse-engineer from a worn sample
